RideShare Saver - Split the Ride, Save the Cash.

Stop paying full price to drive to uni alone. Team up with your classmates on your route.

RideShare Saver matches students commuting to the same campus so they can split fuel and parking costs, cutting the price of getting to class without giving up flexibility.
